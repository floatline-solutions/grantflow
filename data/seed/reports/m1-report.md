# Milestone 1 report - Baseline survey and enumerator training

Submitted by: Dr. Fatou Ndiaye, PI, Institut de Sante et Developpement (UCAD)
Period: 10 October - 20 November 2025

## Summary

All milestone-1 activities are complete. We trained fourteen enumerators (12 were required), translated and pre-tested the instrument, drew the sample frame, obtained ethics clearance and planned the field work by district.

## Training

Enumerator training took place at Hotel Le Baobab, Kaolack, from 3 to 7 November 2025. Fourteen enumerators attended all five days; the signed attendance sheet is attached as Annex A (scan, 3 pages). Two candidates dropped out after day one and are not counted.

## Instrument

The household questionnaire was translated into Wolof by the co-operative's senior enumerators and back-translated by an independent linguist. The Wolof version was pre-tested with 30 respondents in two non-sample villages (Ndoffane and Gandiaye) on 12-13 November; the pre-test led to 11 wording changes, listed in Annex B.

## Sampling

Using the 2023 census enumeration-area list, we drew a baseline sample frame of 600 households (300 per region) with probability proportional to size in the first stage and systematic selection in the second stage. Sampling weights are in the attached `sample_frame_v3.csv` (columns: ea_id, hh_id, region, weight).

## Ethics

The CNERS (Comite National d'Ethique pour la Recherche en Sante) approval letter, reference 000187/MSAS/CNERS, dated 29 October 2025, is attached as Annex C.

## Field plan

Data collection is planned as follows: Kaolack department 24 November - 12 December 2025; Fatick department 15 December 2025 - 9 January 2026 (with a break 24 December - 2 January). Team composition and daily targets are in Annex D.
