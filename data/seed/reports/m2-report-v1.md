# Milestone 2 report (draft 1) - Baseline data collection

Submitted by: Dr. Fatou Ndiaye, PI
Period: 24 November 2025 - 9 January 2026

## Summary

Data collection is finished in both regions and we are pleased with the field teams' work despite the holiday break and two vehicle breakdowns.

## Coverage

The survey was completed with 561 households out of the 600 sampled (93.5% response rate). Refusals were concentrated in two enumeration areas near Fatick town; replacement households were not used, per protocol.

## Data

The raw data are stored on the project server. Cleaning is ongoing and the cleaned dataset will be delivered together with the codebook in the next two weeks. Duplicates were found for 7 households and are being resolved with the supervisors.

## Preliminary findings

Early tabulations suggest that 38% of households had contact with a community health worker in the last three months, with large variation between districts. A two-page summary will follow once cleaning is complete.

## Finances

Invoices for the period are collected and will be reconciled with the ledger in the interim financial report.
