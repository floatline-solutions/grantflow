# Milestone 2 report (revised) - Baseline data collection and cleaned dataset

Submitted by: Dr. Fatou Ndiaye, PI
Period: 24 November 2025 - 24 January 2026
Revision note: addresses the reviewer comments of 12 January (missing dataset, data-quality evidence and financial reconciliation).

## Coverage

The baseline survey was completed with 561 of the 600 sampled households, a 93.5% response rate, above the 90% target. Refusals and unreachable households are listed by enumeration area in Annex A.

## Cleaned dataset and codebook

The cleaned dataset is delivered as `chw_baseline_clean_v1.csv` (561 rows, 214 variables) together with the codebook `chw_baseline_codebook_v1.pdf`, which documents every variable, its question text in French and Wolof, value labels and missing codes. The 7 duplicate households were resolved by keeping the interview with the later timestamp.

## Data quality report

Back-checks were carried out on 58 interviews (10.3% of the sample) by supervisors using a 12-question short form; the agreement rate was 96% and two enumerators were retrained. GPS coordinates were captured for 557 interviews; the coverage map by enumeration area is Annex B. Four interviews without GPS were completed indoors after device failure and are flagged in the dataset.

## Interim financial report

The interim financial report (Annex C) reconciles the on-chain category ledger of the wallet with the vendor invoices: personnel 5,999.99, equipment 2,500.00, fieldwork 2,000.00, training 1,175.50 and overhead 800.00 USDC, a total of 12,475.49 against the 12,500.00 tranche. Two attempted payments were refused by the wallet policy (one over the equipment cap, one to a non-allowlisted vendor) and are documented with the vendors' revised invoices.

## Preliminary findings

A two-page summary of preliminary findings is attached as Annex D. Thirty-eight percent of households reported contact with a community health worker in the previous three months, ranging from 21% in Fatick department to 52% in Kaolack department.
